# Code of conduct

WARLOGS is a small hobby project. Everyone who files an issue, opens a pull request or
comments here is expected to:

- be respectful and assume good intent, including toward new players and new contributors;
- keep feedback about the code, the app and the game, not about people;
- not harass, insult or demean anyone, publicly or privately;
- not post other people's personal information (Steam IDs, real names, screenshots of
  their profiles) without their permission.

The maintainer may edit or remove comments, issues and pull requests that break these
rules, and may block people who keep breaking them.

To report a problem privately, use **Report a vulnerability** on the repository's
Security tab (it reaches only the maintainer), or contact bc1224 on GitHub.

This is based on the spirit of the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
